What your assistant does

Built for the work
you keep postponing.

Not a search engine. Not a chatbot that needs hand-holding. An assistant configured to your business that handles real work — autonomously.

Drafts your weekly report

Pulls from your connected tools — calendar, docs, project tracker — and writes the status update. You review and send. Five minutes, not fifty.

Monitors your competitors

Tracks pricing pages, job boards, press releases, and product announcements. Surfaces relevant changes to your Slack channel on a schedule you set.

Handles inbox triage

Reads, categorizes, and prioritizes. Drafts replies for your review. Flags urgent threads. Quietly processes the rest. You touch what actually needs you.

Books and manages your calendar

Message it in natural language. "Find 30 minutes with Sarah this week." It handles the back-and-forth, sends the invite, adds context you'd forget to include.

Tracks your KPIs and alerts you

Connects to your data sources. Monitors the numbers that matter to you. Sends a message when something moves meaningfully — up or down.

Briefs you before every meeting

Reads the calendar, pulls context from past conversations and docs, and sends you a short brief five minutes before each call. You walk in prepared.

Responds to your team instantly

Available in Slack 24/7. Your team asks questions and gets answers — about processes, documents, previous decisions — without waiting for you.

Drafts decks, docs, and proposals

Give it context and a goal. Get back a structured first draft — slide outline, pitch doc, job description, whatever you need. Edit instead of starting blank.

Runs your recurring workflows

Weekly reporting cycles, monthly invoicing summaries, onboarding sequences — configure it once. It handles the repetition so your team handles everything else.
